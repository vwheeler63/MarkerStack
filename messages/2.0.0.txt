Version 2.0.0 (2026-03-24 09:56)
--------------------------------

- Enhanced scroll state when a pushed position is popped:  now the scroll state
  restored is the relative vertical position of the caret within the Viewport,
  replacing the previous behavior which was the raw number of pixels the Viewport
  was scrolled down from the top of the text when the position was pushed.
- Minor enhancements to documentation.
